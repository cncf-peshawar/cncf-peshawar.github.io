Peshawar is home to a rapidly growing pool of technology talent, developers, engineers, and students eager to build the future of cloud-native computing. Yet despite this immense potential, the city has historically lacked a structured, active community group dedicated to advancing open-source and cloud-native technologies.

**Cloud Native Peshawar exists to change that.**

## Our Mission

Our mission is to bridge the gap between global cloud-native innovation and local talent by building an inclusive, vendor-neutral, and hands-on community where practitioners of all skill levels can learn, collaborate, and grow.

We are committed to democratizing knowledge around **Kubernetes, containers, microservices, DevOps, MLOps, and Agentic AI**—technologies shaping the modern infrastructure landscape.
