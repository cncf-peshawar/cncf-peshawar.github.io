## About This Event

**CNCF Peshawar Genesis**, proudly sponsored by **GitHub** and **nsave**, marks the beginning of the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) community in Peshawar, bringing together developers, engineers, students, and technology enthusiasts passionate about cloud-native technologies and open source.

The event is designed to be more than just a series of talks. It combines:
- **Expert Technical Sessions**
- **Interactive Activities & Rapid-Fire Panel**
- **Technical Challenges**
- **Live Kahoot Quiz & Scavenger Hunt**
- **Community Networking & Collaborations**

As the inaugural gathering of CNCF Peshawar, Genesis aims to create meaningful connections, spark new ideas, and provide opportunities for individuals to learn, build, and grow together.
