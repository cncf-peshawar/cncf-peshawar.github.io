## About This Event

MLH x CNP Hack Day Peshawar 2026 📅 Saturday, 21 November 2026 🕙 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM 📍 FAST NUCES, Peshawar Campus Cloud Native Peshawar, a CNCF community chapter, is teaming up with Major League Hacking (MLH) to bring a one-day hackathon to Peshawar. This isn’t about building a demo that gets forgotten next week. It’s about building an AI product MVP that solves a real problem and could become a startup. Bring your team, your idea, and your laptop. In one day you’ll build a working AI product, pitch it to judges, and connect with 150+ students, developers, and founders from across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Who should join? Students from any university or background Developers, designers, and anyone curious about AI Aspiring founders with a startup idea Beginners are welcome. You don’t need to be an expert to build something great. Challenges 🏆 Best Use of the Google Gemini API Build an AI-powered app with Gemini: chatbots, summarizers, creative tools, or anything that makes people say WHOA. Prize: 10,000 PKR. 🚀 Best AI Startup MVP Build a market-ready AI MVP with Google technologies that solves a real problem, can scale, and has a clear business model. Judged on problem and impact, market potential, use of Google tech, scalability, and pitch. Schedule 10:00 AM: Registration and check-in 10:30 AM: Opening ceremony and challenge briefing 11:00 AM: Hacking begins 1:00 PM: Lunch break 2:30 PM: Hacking ends, submissions close 2:45 PM: Project demos and judging 3:30 PM: Closing ceremony and winners announced 4:00 PM: Networking and wrap-up Rules Teams of 2 to 4 members All code must be written during the event You can use open source libraries, APIs, and frameworks Submit a demo (live link or video), your code repository, and a short pitch Follow the MLH Code of Conduct: mlh.io/code-of-conduct What to bring Laptop and charger Your university ID or CNIC Your team and your best ideas What you get Cash prizes for winners MLH swag Mentorship from developers and AI engineers Food and refreshments Networking with Peshawar’s tech and startup community Organized by Cloud Native Peshawar (CNCF / Linux Foundation) in partnership with Major League Hacking Seats are limited. Register now and build what’s next. 🚀
