# Snow Leopard Mascot — Concept Sheet

## Rationale
Snow leopards are native to northern Pakistan (Hunza, Khunjerab).
They symbolize agility, rarity, and power — fitting for a cloud-native
community that values precision and rare talent.

## Design language
- Geometric but friendly (not photorealistic)
- Rosette spots as small circles/squares (Kubernetes-pod-like)
- Snow-white belly, charcoal-grey rosettes, sky-blue eyes
- 2-3px stroke weight, 2px radius, hand-drawn feel
- No CNCF project logos reproduced; no Kubernetes wheel

## Three poses
1. Standing — confident, facing forward (hero/landing use)
2. Sitting — attentive, tail curled (footer/sidebar use)
3. Leaping — dynamic, mid-jump (CTA/transition use)

## Colour usage
- Body: #ffffff
- Rosettes: #383838
- Eyes: #54b4de
- Optional accent (collar/tag): #ffde00
