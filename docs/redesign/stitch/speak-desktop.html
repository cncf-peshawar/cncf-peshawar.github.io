01 // TALK FORMATS

Session Formats We Host.

Choose the session structure that matches your topic depth and comfort level.

15 MINUTES

Lightning Talk

Fast-paced, focused introduction to a single open-source tool, bug fix story, or lesson learned in production. Ideal for first-time speakers!

  • Single concept focus
  • Live demo optional
  • No audience Q&A required
40 MINUTES

Technical Deep Dive

In-depth architectural breakdown, code walkthrough, or real-world migration case study with 5-10 minutes of audience Q&A.

  • Architecture patterns
  • Production trade-offs
  • Live Q&A session
90 MINUTES

Hands-on Workshop

Step-by-step interactive lab where attendees follow along on their own laptops to deploy, configure, or debug infrastructure.

  • Pre-configured repo
  • Hands-on attendee tasks
  • Co-mentors assist in room
02 // SPEAKER SUPPORT

We Support First-Time Speakers.

You don't need prior public speaking experience. Our organizing squad assists you at every milestone.

01

Proposal Review

02

Slide Dry-Runs

Practice your slides and live terminal demo with an organizer in a private video call.

03

Equipment & AV

High-res projection, wireless presenter, clicker, lapel mic, and high-speed Wi-Fi provided.

04

Speaker Honor

Exclusive speaker swag, published bio on the chapter portal, and community recognition.
