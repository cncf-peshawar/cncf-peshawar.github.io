UPCOMING MEETUP MEETUP #01 // GENESIS

Cloud Native Peshawar Genesis: KubeCon Recap & Local Cloud-Native Kickoff

Official kickoff meetup for the CNCF Peshawar community. Deep dive into KubeCon NA highlights, live demo of local Kubernetes tooling, and community networking.

About This Meetup

Welcome to the inaugural gathering of Cloud Native Peshawar! We are excited to bring together local software engineers, DevOps practitioners, platform builders, students, and researchers to inaugurate our official CNCF chapter in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Whether you are running production Kubernetes clusters or just wrote your very first Dockerfile, this meetup is structured to give you practical, vendor-neutral insights into modern distributed systems and container orchestration.

Session Agenda

  • 03:00 PM – 03:30 PM: Check-in, Welcome Chai & Community Networking
  • 03:30 PM – 04:15 PM: KubeCon North America Recap: The State of Cloud Native in 2026
  • 04:15 PM – 05:15 PM: Hands-on Lab: Zero-to-Cluster with kind, Cilium, and Helm
  • 05:15 PM – 05:45 PM: Community Open Mic & Lightning Talks
  • 05:45 PM – 06:30 PM: Swag Distribution, Q&A, and Closing Chai

Prerequisites & What to Bring

To follow along with the hands-on lab, please bring a laptop with:

  • Docker Desktop or Colima installed and running
  • kubectl CLI installed
  • Git and an active GitHub account

Venue Location Note

IM|Sciences Hall B, Phase 7, Hayatabad, Peshawar. Secure parking and wheelchair-accessible entrance available.
