## 6. Deviations ($\le 3$)

1. **Breadcrumb Top Back Link:** In addition to the bottom back button on `/team/[group]`, a top back link button (`← Back to All Teams`) is placed next to the breadcrumbs matching `team-group-desktop.html`, providing instant navigation for users without requiring scrolling past long member rosters.
2. **Rotating Sketch Accents on Working Group Cards:** Each working group card features a dedicated 6px left border accent corresponding to the MotherDuck rainbow sketch palette: Lilac for Tech Team (`WG-01`), Orange for Events Team (`WG-02`), Mint for Community Outreach (`WG-03`), and Periwinkle for Design & Media (`WG-04`).
3. **Interactive 44px Tap Targets for Social Badges:** Social icon links and LF ID badges feature minimum 44px tap targets and high-contrast `--focus-ring` outlines, exceeding standard mobile accessibility criteria.
